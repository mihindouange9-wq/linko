# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript, pinned by the brief. GSAP + ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography and physical motion; CSS for simple interactions; WebGL only where it serves the connection concept, with a clean fallback. Each library must justify itself.

## Users

- **People and households** who have a concrete need (a leak, a breakdown, a wall to paint, a garden, a broken laptop) and want the right professional quickly, without asking around.
- **Companies** that need a skill fast (maintenance, refrigeration, IT, cleaning, welding) for a site, an office or a one-off intervention.
- **Skilled professionals** (plumbers, electricians, painters, masons, carpenters, tilers, welders, mechanics, refrigeration technicians, technicians, IT specialists, gardeners, cleaners) who want to be found, show their work and win new clients.

Market: launch in **Gabon** (Libreville first, then Port-Gentil, Franceville); French-speaking Africa afterwards. Interface language: French. Currency: franc CFA (XAF).

## Product Purpose

LINKO is a skills marketplace. Someone describes a need; LINKO surfaces the available professionals able to meet it, and lets them compare each one's characteristics: **diplomas and certifications (verified)**, experience, ratings, **availability (agenda with bookable slots)** and location, then connect. Professionals hold a profile with skills, diplomas, experience, past work, service area, availability, ratings and verification status. The app must be simple: search a worker → see each one's characteristics → contact. Success: a need becomes a confirmed connection with the right person.

## Positioning

LINKO is not "a directory of workers": it is the link between **a need** and **a skill**. Brand line: **« Un besoin. La bonne compétence. »** The product's mechanism is matching: separation → search → filtering → match → connection → stabilization.

## Operating Context

Users mostly search from a phone, often under time pressure (something is broken). Professionals manage availability and profiles on mobile. Companies search from desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Need description / search, category browsing, profile comparison, ratings, availability, location, verification status, connection.
- Pro side: profile, skills, portfolio, service area, availability, reviews.
- Business side: fast access to required skills.
- Business model: free for clients; professionals pay a monthly subscription to be listed and visible. Launch prices proposed on 2026-10-05 (to be confirmed by the user): Essentiel 3 000 FCFA/month (30 000/year), Pro 8 000 FCFA/month (80 000/year), first month free, payment by Airtel Money / Moov Money / card. Benchmarks used: Gabon SMIG 80 000 FCFA, RMM 150 000 FCFA.
- Undecided: commission on interventions (none planned), app store release date.

## Brand Commitments

- Name LINKO; slogan « Un besoin. La bonne compétence. »
- Existing logo: `Logo linko — connexion et compétences.png` — two interlocking shapes (green and obsidian), each a dot plus a hooked body, joining as one; lowercase geometric wordmark with a green leaf-shaped dot on the i.
- Palette (binding): Obsidian #111315, Off White #F5F4EF, Link Green #20C77A, Deep Green #087A4B, Slate #667078, Soft Grey #E8EAE7. Green is a signal (connection, availability, validation, active state, strategic CTA), never the structure; obsidian and off-white carry the structure.
- Logo must not depict tools, houses, workers, buildings or handshakes.
- The user wants the app illustrated on **iPhone** frames on the site (pinned on 2026-10-05; this overrides the generic phone-mockup ban for the app chapters). Frames are drawn in CSS, no raster.
- Explicit bans from the brief: blobs, multicolor/aurora gradients, glassmorphism, generic floating cards, random text animation, artificial horizontal scroll, zooming giant words, generic 3D/spheres, decorative particles, gadget cursors, systematic parallax, one-by-one card reveals, fade/slide-up everywhere, infinite marquee, random worker photos.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo PNG (above). Nothing else.
- No real figures, testimonials, partners, or live app yet. All profiles, reviews and numbers shown are demonstration data and must be labelled as such. Never invent user counts, coverage claims, prices or partners.

## Product Principles

1. Every motion explains the connection between a need and a skill; if it explains nothing, it does not move.
2. Speed to the right person beats browsing breadth.
3. Trust is shown, not claimed: verification, reviews, experience and availability are visible facts.
4. Mobile first in use, never a shrunken desktop.
5. Calm → precise → intelligent → alive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Full prefers-reduced-motion path. Readable on mid-range Android phones and slower networks; the experience must work without WebGL.
